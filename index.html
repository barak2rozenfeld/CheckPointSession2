What a good CISO asks next

The hard questions, answered first

Policy miss?Same rule, same outcome

One rule base stays in SmartConsole. The card runs the inspection. If a block, a log or an exception differs from the CPU path, turn offload off and the Xeon enforces again. Pull the card only if you must. If the card fails on its own, inspection returns to that CPU path, at lower throughput.

Lock-in?You are not married to NVIDIA

An abstraction layer in GAiA sits between your policy and the chip. Generation 2 can target Intel IPU or AMD Pensando without touching your policy. The lock-in to fear is an engine burned into a fixed ASIC, which is the opposite of a card.

No card?The 9800 is great without it

Everything you saw on "Where you are" is true today, with no card. The slot is upside, never a dependency. If the program slips, nothing about the box you own changes.

When?It is a roadmap, with gates

Proposed path: kickoff Q1 '27, PoC gate Q3 '27, beta Q1 '28, GA Q3 '28. Every date after the first has a gate in front of it. If the PoC misses the target, the program does not proceed.

Why me?A design-partner seat

As an existing 9800 customer you are the ideal design partner: early hardware, input on requirements, and your traffic profile becomes a design input. You shape it, you do not just wait for it.
